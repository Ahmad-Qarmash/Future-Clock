Everything you need in a clock

Five first-class tools, one cohesive neon experience.

  • Clock

    Large neon digits with ticking seconds, date, day, battery and timezone — all at a glance.

  • World Clock

    Add any of 150+ curated cities with automatic day-difference indicators and live local time.

  • Alarms

    Repeat by day, custom label, gradual volume, vibration, snooze, and a math challenge to force wake-up.

  • Timer

    Circular neon progress, presets (1/3/5/10/30 min), foreground service, system notification with pause/reset.

  • Stopwatch

    Sub-second precision, lap history, share via system intent. Backgrounds cleanly with a foreground service.

Four home-screen widgets

Production-quality widgets that tick every minute, share the same renderer, and respect your battery.

  • Analog

    Cyberpunk clock face with neon hands.

  • Digital

    Bold neon HH:MM with day and date.

  • World

    1–3 cities, each on local time.

  • Next Alarm

    Your next alarm at a glance.
